## Problem

What can't be done today? Who is affected, and how often? Describe the
situation, not the solution — the solution is the next stage's job.

## Proposed outcome

What does better look like, from the outside? Someone who never reads the code
should be able to tell whether this was achieved.

## Affected users and systems

be incomplete; the design pass will find the rest.

## Constraints

Anything that must stay true. Budgets, policies, things that must not break.
Also worth stating what you are deliberately *not* constraining, so the design
pass knows where it has room.

## Open questions

Things you are unsure about. Leaving these unresolved is correct — a question
carried forward honestly is better than an answer invented to fill a heading.

## Out of scope
